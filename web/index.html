Use the worksheet well

Use an actual rental listing or mortgage estimate and the actual job offer. Add vehicle ownership, child care, insurance and any recurring costs that differ. For hourly pay, use paid hours per week and paid weeks per year; unpaid time off matters. All amounts are Canadian dollars.

Assumptions and privacy

The worksheet uses fixed inputs. It does not estimate taxes, investment returns, future inflation, relocation benefits, home appreciation or employment probability. Commuting time is optional context, not an expense unless you enter its monetary cost.

All scenario calculations happen on your device. No inputs or imported files are uploaded. Explicitly saved data stays in this browser until deleted. The app retrieves only its public configuration and regional reference snapshot. The shared CoolCode counter counts tool opens without financial inputs.
